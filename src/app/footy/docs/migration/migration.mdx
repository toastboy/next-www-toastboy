# Migrating to the New Footy Site

Welcome to the new toastboy.co.uk footy site. This document covers what has changed
from the old site and what you need to do to get started.

## Your history is already here

All your existing game history, stats and results have already been carried
over — you don't need to re-enter anything. What's new is how you sign in.

## Setting up your login

If an admin has sent you a link (usually by email), follow it to set up your
login — you'll be asked to choose how you'd like to sign in (Google,
Microsoft, or an email address and password) and confirm your name and
email. Once you finish, you're taken straight to your profile and your new
login is linked to your existing player record — your history follows you
automatically.

That link is single-use and expires after a while. If yours has stopped
working, ask an admin for a new one.

If you haven't been sent a link yet, get in touch with an admin and they can
send you one.

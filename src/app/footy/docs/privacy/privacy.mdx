# Toastboy FC — Privacy Notice

Last updated: 2026-09-08

## 1. Who we are

Toastboy FC ("we", "us", "the club") runs a friendly five-a-side football league
and the website at [www.toastboy.co.uk](https://www.toastboy.co.uk), which we
use to organise games, record results and player statistics, and manage members'
game payments.

For the purposes of UK data protection law, the data controller is:

- **Jon Wilson, on behalf of Toastboy FC**
- Contact: **[privacy@toastboy.co.uk](mailto:privacy@toastboy.co.uk)**

If you have any questions about this notice or how we handle your personal data,
contact us at the address above.

## 2. What personal data we collect

Depending on how you interact with the club and the site, we may hold:

- **Identity and contact** — your name, email address(es).
- **Optional profile details** — year of birth, free-text notes you or an
  administrator add, which existing member introduced you.
- **Participation data** — your responses to game invitations, which games you
  played, team allocations, whether you came to the pub.
- **Player ratings** — skill ratings you give other players and that others give
  you, shown anonymously to players but linked to accounts in our database.
- **Financial records** — game charges, payments you have made, and your running
  balance with the club.
- **Messages** — match-thread chat messages you post, and any message you send
  us through the contact form.
- **Account and technical data** — the login method you use (Google, Microsoft,
  or email), session information including IP address and browser type, and
  authentication tokens from your chosen login provider.

We do **not** collect special category data (such as health, ethnicity,
religious or political views) and we do **not** hold your payment card details —
payments are arranged directly with the organiser.

## 3. Why we use your data, and our lawful basis

- **Organising games, recording results, statistics and team selection** —
  legitimate interests: running the league you have joined.
- **Managing your balance and recording payments** — legitimate interests, and a
  legal obligation to keep basic financial records.
- **Sending you game invitations and replies** — legitimate interests: you have
  asked to be included in games.
- **Occasional club-wide emails about club matters** — legitimate interests
  (news relevant to current members); you can opt out at any time.
- **Letting you sign in** — necessary to provide the service you have requested,
  via the login provider you choose.
- **Responding to messages you send us** — legitimate interests: answering your
  enquiry.
- **Keeping the site secure and diagnosing errors** — legitimate interests:
  protecting the site and its users.

Where we rely on legitimate interests, we have weighed them against your rights
and would not expect our use of your data to surprise or harm you. You can
object at any time (see section 8).

## 4. Who we share it with

We do not sell your data or share it for marketing. We use a small number of
service providers ("processors") who handle data on our behalf:

- **Google and Microsoft** — sign-in, if you choose one of them as your login
  provider.
- **Microsoft 365 (Graph)** — sending email from the club.
- **Microsoft Azure** — database hosting and file/image storage.
- **Sentry** — error monitoring.

Some providers may process data outside the UK. Where they do, transfers are
covered by UK "adequacy" regulations or standard contractual clauses giving
equivalent protection.

We will also disclose data where required by law.

## 5. How long we keep it

- **Your profile and participation history:** kept for as long as the club
  operates, so that historical results, statistics and league tables stay
  accurate. We do not automatically delete or anonymise accounts after a period
  of inactivity.
- **Anonymising your profile:** at your request, or at an administrator's
  discretion, your profile can be marked as anonymous. Your name and email
  address are then hidden and you appear publicly only as "Player" followed by a
  number. This applies whether or not you are still an active player. The
  underlying game records are retained but are no longer publicly linked to you.
- **Deleting your data:** you can go further and ask us to erase your personal
  data entirely. We will remove your name, email address(es) and any notes we
  hold about you. Where a game record cannot be deleted without breaking
  historical results and league tables, we keep only the minimum needed for
  those figures to remain accurate, with nothing that identifies you.
- **Email verification links and login tokens:** expire quickly and are deleted
  automatically.
- **Aggregate statistics** that do not identify you may be kept indefinitely.

## 6. How we protect your data

- All traffic to the site uses HTTPS.
- Both the live database and its backups are access-controlled and reachable
  only through least-privilege accounts.
- Members' contact and financial details are visible only to the organiser and
  any appointed administrators.

## 7. Cookies

We use a single essential cookie to keep you signed in. We do not use
advertising or third-party tracking cookies.

## 8. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

- **access** the data we hold about you — much of it is available from the
  "Download my data" page when signed in;
- ask us to **correct** inaccurate data;
- ask us to **erase** your data — note we may keep anonymised historical
  results;
- ask us to **restrict** or **object to** our processing;
- **data portability** — receive your data in a machine-readable format;
- **withdraw consent** where we rely on it.

To exercise any of these, contact us at
[privacy@toastboy.co.uk](mailto:privacy@toastboy.co.uk). We will respond within
one month.

## 9. Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, please tell us first so
we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information
Commissioner's Office (ICO): [www.ico.org.uk](https://www.ico.org.uk) · 0303 123
1113 · Information Commissioner's Office, Wycliffe House, Water Lane, Wilmslow,
Cheshire SK9 5AF.

We may update this notice from time to time. The "last updated" date shows when
it last changed; significant changes will be notified by email or on the site.
